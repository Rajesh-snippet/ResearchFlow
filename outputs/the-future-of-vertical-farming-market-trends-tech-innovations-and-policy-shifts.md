## Sustainability and Climate Resilience Benefits  

Vertical farms achieve dramatic water savings by recirculating hydroponic solutions, often quoted as a **70‑90 % reduction** compared with field irrigation. *Not found in provided sources.*  

When powered by renewable electricity, the **lifecycle carbon footprint can be up to 60 % lower** than that of conventional crops, thanks to eliminated tillage, fertilizer volatilization, and transport emissions. *Not found in provided sources.*  

Locating farms inside or near urban centers slashes “food‑miles,” strengthening supply‑chain resilience against climate‑induced disruptions and reducing reliance on long‑haul logistics. *Not found in provided sources.*  

Many operators adopt circular‑economy loops, converting plant residues and spent media into bio‑fertilizers or compost, thereby closing nutrient cycles and cutting waste disposal impacts. *Not found in provided sources.*
